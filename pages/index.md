Hey! I'm Syofyan Zuhad, a fanatical open sourceror and web artisan.

I'm a freelance <a href="//linkedin.com/in/syofyan-zuhad" target="_blank">web developer</a>, focused on building web applications with <a href="//github.com/laravel" target="_blank">Laravel</a>.

I enjoy turning ideas into useful tools, especially those that make everyday work simpler, more productive, or just more enjoyable. I build things for clients, experiment with my own ideas, and maintain open-source projects such as <a href="//uptime.syofyanzuhad.dev" target="_blank">Uptime Kita</a>, <a href="//katla.syofyanzuhad.dev" target="_blank">Katla</a>, and <a href="//hobits.syofyanzuhad.dev" target="_blank">Hobits</a>. You can find my [full projects list here](/projects).

I also enjoy sharing what I learn. I give [talks](/giving-talks), write <a href="//syofyanzuhad.medium.com" target="_blank">blog posts</a>, and <a href="//adplist.org/mentors/syofyan-zuhad" target="_blank">mentor</a> developers who are learning web development, Laravel, and software engineering. If you're looking for guidance on building projects, improving your development skills, or navigating your journey as a developer, feel free to [reach out](/contact).

Outside of programming, I enjoy photography and traveling. I post [photos on this page](/photos) and <a href="//instagram.com/randomsh_ts" target="_blank">my Instagram</a>. I also love anime, movies, and dramas, and I'm trying to keep track of my [media consumption](/media). You can also see the [hardware and software I use](/uses).

I usually stay in Purwokerto or Jakarta, Indonesia. If you're around, feel free to reach out and let's have some coffee or work together.

You can also take a look at <a href="/syofyan-zuhad.pdf" target="_blank">my CV</a>.

P.S. This website is open-source and available on <a href="//github.com/syofyanzuhad/syofyanzuhad.dev" target="_blank">GitHub</a>.
